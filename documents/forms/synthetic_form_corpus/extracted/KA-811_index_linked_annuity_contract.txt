DEFINITIONS
Buffer means the percentage of negative Index Performance that we will absorb at the end of an Index Term before any
negative Index Credit is applied to a Strategy Account.
Cap means the maximum percentage of positive Index Performance that may be credited to a Strategy Account at the end of
an Index Term.
Contract Value means the sum of the values of all Strategy Accounts and the Fixed Account as of any Business Day.
Index Credit means the amount, positive or negative, applied to a Strategy Account at the end of an Index Term, determined
by the crediting method applicable to that Strategy Account.
Index Performance means the percentage change in the Index Value from the first day of an Index Term to the last day of
that Index Term.
Index Term means the period during which Index Performance is measured for a Strategy Account, as shown on the
Contract Data Page.
Interim Value means the value of a Strategy Account on any Business Day other than the first or last day of an Index Term,
determined by the formula set forth in this Contract.
Strategy Account means an account to which you allocate all or a portion of your Contract Value, the value of which is
determined in part by reference to an Index.

STRATEGY ACCOUNT PROVISIONS
Allocation
On the Contract Date, we will allocate your Purchase Payment among the Strategy Accounts and the Fixed Account according to
your instructions. We must receive allocation instructions in a form acceptable to us. Any portion of the Purchase Payment for
which we have not received instructions will be allocated to the Fixed Account until we receive acceptable instructions.
Index Credit Calculation
At the end of each Index Term, we will calculate an Index Credit for each Strategy Account. If Index Performance is positive, the
Index Credit will equal Index Performance multiplied by any applicable Participation Rate, but will not exceed any applicable
Cap. If Index Performance is negative, the Index Credit will equal zero to the extent the negative Index Performance does not
exceed the Buffer. To the extent negative Index Performance exceeds the Buffer, the Index Credit will be negative and equal to
that portion of the negative Index Performance in excess of the Buffer. You may lose a substantial portion of your investment as a
result of a negative Index Credit.
Interim Value
If you take a withdrawal, surrender the Contract, or a death benefit becomes payable on any day other than the last day of an
Index Term, we will determine the value of each affected Strategy Account using the Interim Value. The Interim Value may be
less than the amount allocated to the Strategy Account even if Index Performance has been positive since the beginning of the
Index Term. The Interim Value calculation uses the fair value of a replicating portfolio of hypothetical derivative instruments and
is described in the Interim Value Appendix to this Contract.
Index Substitution
If an Index is discontinued, or if the calculation of an Index is substantially changed, or if our right to use an Index is terminated,
we may substitute a comparable Index. We will obtain any required regulatory approval before substituting an Index and will
notify you at least thirty days before the substitution takes effect.
WITHDRAWAL AND SURRENDER PROVISIONS
You may withdraw all or part of the Contract Value at any time before the Maturity Date while the Annuitant is living. Each
withdrawal must be at least $500. A Withdrawal Charge will be assessed on any amount withdrawn in excess of the Free
Withdrawal Amount during the Withdrawal Charge Period, calculated using the schedule shown on the Contract Data Page. We
will not assess a Withdrawal Charge on amounts withdrawn to satisfy required minimum distributions under the Internal Revenue
Code, or on any amount payable as a death benefit.
DEATH BENEFIT PROVISIONS
If the Owner dies before the Maturity Date, we will pay a death benefit to the Beneficiary upon receipt at our Service Center of
proof of death and instructions regarding payment. The death benefit will equal the greater of the Contract Value determined as
of the date we receive proof of death and payment instructions, or the Purchase Payment reduced proportionally for any
withdrawals. We will pay interest on the death benefit from the date of death as required by the law of the state in which this
Contract is delivered.
