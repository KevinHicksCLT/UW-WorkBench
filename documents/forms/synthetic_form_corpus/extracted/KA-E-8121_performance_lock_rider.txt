1. PURPOSE OF THIS RIDER
This Rider adds the Lock Strategy Accounts described in the Rider Data above to your Contract. A Lock Strategy Account
automatically locks and credits actual Index Performance before the end of an Index Term if the Lock Threshold is met on any
Business Day during the Index Term.
2. DEFINITIONS ADDED BY THIS RIDER
Lock Threshold means the level of cumulative positive Index Performance, measured from the first day of the Index Term,
which if attained on any Business Day will trigger a Lock Event for the applicable Lock Strategy Account.
Lock Event means the automatic crediting of the Locked Index Credit to a Lock Strategy Account, which occurs on the first
Business Day on which Index Performance for that Strategy Account equals or exceeds the Lock Threshold.
Locked Index Credit means the Index Credit determined and applied upon a Lock Event, equal to the Index Performance
measured on the date of the Lock Event, which will not be less than the Lock Threshold.
3. LOCK EVENT
We will monitor Index Performance for each Lock Strategy Account on each Business Day during the Index Term using the
closing Index Value. If on any Business Day the Index Performance equals or exceeds the Lock Threshold shown in the Rider
Data, a Lock Event will occur. Upon a Lock Event, we will credit the Locked Index Credit to the Lock Strategy Account. No
further Index Credit, positive or negative, will be applied to that Lock Strategy Account for the remainder of the Index Term, and
the value of that Lock Strategy Account will not be affected by subsequent Index Performance.
We will notify you in writing within ten Business Days after a Lock Event occurs. A Lock Event occurs automatically and does
not require any action or election by you. You may not elect to lock or to decline to lock.
4. IF NO LOCK EVENT OCCURS
If no Lock Event occurs during an Index Term, we will determine the Index Credit at the end of the Index Term in accordance
with the terms of the Contract, applying the Buffer shown in the Rider Data. You may receive a negative Index Credit and may
lose a substantial portion of the amount allocated to the Lock Strategy Account.
5. WITHDRAWALS AND INTERIM VALUE
If you take a withdrawal from a Lock Strategy Account after a Lock Event but before the end of the Index Term, the amount
available will be determined using the Locked Index Credit and will not be subject to further Interim Value adjustment based on
Index Performance. If you take a withdrawal before a Lock Event occurs, the Interim Value provisions of the Contract will apply
and the Interim Value may be less than the amount allocated to the Lock Strategy Account.
