 With respect to coverage provided by this endorsement, the provisions of the policy apply unless modified by this endorsement.
A. DEFINITIONS
The following definitions are added to the DEFINITIONS section of the policy:
"Transportation network platform" means an online-enabled application, software, website or system offered or used by
a transportation network company that enables the prearrangement of rides for compensation.
"Transportation network period" means the period of time that begins when a driver logs on to a transportation network
platform and becomes available to receive a request for a prearranged ride, and ends when the driver logs off the platform or
the last passenger has exited the vehicle and the trip has been terminated in the platform, whichever occurs later. A
transportation network period exists whether or not a passenger is occupying the vehicle and whether or not a ride has been
accepted.
B. CHANGES TO PART A - LIABILITY COVERAGE
The following exclusion is added:
We do not provide Liability Coverage for any insured for bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership,
maintenance or use of any vehicle during a transportation network period.
C. CHANGES TO PART B - MEDICAL PAYMENTS COVERAGE
We do not provide Medical Payments Coverage for any insured for bodily injury sustained during a transportation network
period.
D. CHANGES TO PART D - COVERAGE FOR DAMAGE TO YOUR AUTO
We will not pay for any loss to your covered auto or any non-owned auto occurring during a transportation network period,
including loss occurring while the vehicle is unoccupied but logged on to a transportation network platform.
E. NO EFFECT ON PERSONAL USE
This endorsement does not restrict coverage that would otherwise apply while the vehicle is used solely for personal purposes and
no transportation network period is in effect. Participation in a share-the-expense car pool does not constitute a transportation
network period.
All other terms and conditions of the policy remain unchanged.
