# GDPR Article Quick Reference (SDLC-relevant)

One line per article. Full obligations in `../../source/gdpr-reference.md`; authoritative text at
EUR-Lex CELEX 32016R0679.

## Scope & definitions
- **Art. 2** — Material scope: automated processing of personal data + filing systems.
- **Art. 3** — Territorial scope: EU establishment, or targeting/monitoring EU data subjects.
- **Art. 4** — Definitions (personal data, processing, controller, processor, consent, breach, profiling).

## Principles
- **Art. 5** — Six principles + accountability (5(2): must *demonstrate* compliance).
- **Art. 6** — Lawful basis (one of six required per purpose).
- **Art. 7** — Consent must be freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous, withdrawable.
- **Art. 9** — Special-category data (health/biometric/etc.) prohibited unless 9(2) condition.
- **Art. 10** — Criminal-offence data restricted.

## Data subject rights
- **Art. 12** — Transparency + respond within one month.
- **Art. 13/14** — Information at collection (from subject / from elsewhere).
- **Art. 15** — Right of access (DSAR).
- **Art. 16** — Rectification.
- **Art. 17** — Erasure; **Art. 19** — propagate erasure/rectification to recipients.
- **Art. 18** — Restriction.
- **Art. 20** — Portability (structured, machine-readable).
- **Art. 21** — Objection (absolute for direct marketing).
- **Art. 22** — No solely-automated decisions with significant effect without safeguards.

## Controller & processor
- **Art. 24** — Controller must implement + demonstrate measures.
- **Art. 25** — Data protection by design & by default.
- **Art. 26** — Joint controllers.
- **Art. 28/29** — Processor obligations + DPAs.
- **Art. 30** — Records of processing activities (RoPA).
- **Art. 32** — Security of processing (encryption, pseudonymisation, CIA, resilience, restore, testing).
- **Art. 33** — Breach notification to authority within 72h.
- **Art. 34** — Breach communication to subjects where high risk.
- **Art. 35** — DPIA for high-risk processing.
- **Art. 36** — Prior consultation where residual risk high.
- **Art. 37–39** — Data Protection Officer designation, position, tasks.

## Transfers
- **Art. 44–49** — Transfers outside EEA: adequacy / SCCs / BCRs / derogations.

## Enforcement
- **Art. 82** — Compensation to data subjects.
- **Art. 83** — Fines: up to €10m/2% (tier 1) or €20m/4% (tier 2) of global turnover, whichever higher.

## Key recitals
- **39** minimisation/transparency · **47** legitimate-interest balancing · **71** automated-decision
  safeguards · **78** by design/default · **83** security risk assessment.
