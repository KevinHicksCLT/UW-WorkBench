# CCPA / CPRA Quick Reference

One line per provision. Full detail in `../../source/ccpa-cpra-reference.md`; authoritative text at
https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/ and https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa.

## Scope & definitions
- **1798.140(d)** — "Business" thresholds: > $25M revenue; or 100,000+ consumers/households bought/sold/shared; or 50%+ revenue from selling/sharing PI.
- **1798.140(v)** — Personal Information (incl. household-level).
- **1798.140(ae)** — Sensitive PI: SSN/IDs, financial credentials, precise geolocation, race/religion/union, private communications, genetic, biometric-ID, health, sex life/orientation.
- **1798.145(e)** — **GLBA exemption** (information-level, not entity-level) — critical for insurers.

## Consumer rights
- **1798.100/.110/.115** — Right to know / access (categories + specific pieces; ≥ 12-month lookback).
- **1798.105** — Right to delete (+ direct service providers to delete).
- **1798.106** — Right to correct.
- **1798.120 / .135** — Opt-out of **sale/sharing** ("sharing" = cross-context behavioral advertising).
- **1798.121** — Right to limit use/disclosure of **Sensitive PI**.
- **11 CCR 7025** — Honor **Global Privacy Control** opt-out preference signals.
- **1798.125** — Non-discrimination; financial-incentive rules.
- **1798.120(c)** — Opt-**in** to sell/share minors' PI (under 16; under 13 parental).
- **ADMT regs** — Notice, opt-out, access, appeal for ADMT in significant decisions (**by 2027-01-01**).

## Request mechanics
- **1798.130 / 11 CCR 7020-7022** — ≥ 2 methods; verify; **ack ≤ 10 business days; respond ≤ 45 days** (+45).

## Business obligations
- **1798.100(a)** — Notice at collection (categories, purposes, retention, sale/share).
- **1798.130 / .135** — Privacy policy, updated every 12 months.
- **1798.100(c)** — Data minimization & purpose limitation (necessary + proportionate).
- **1798.100(a)(3)** — Disclose & enforce retention.
- **1798.100(e)** — Reasonable security. **1798.150** — **breach private right of action** ($100-$750/consumer/incident).
- **1798.100(d) / .140** — Service-provider/contractor/third-party contract terms.

## 2025 CPPA regulations (effective 2026-01-01)
- **Risk assessments** — before high-risk processing (sale/share, SPI, ADMT, training, inference); comply now, **submit to CPPA by 2028-04-01**, then annually.
- **ADMT** — notice/opt-out/access/appeal for tech that substantially replaces human decision-making in significant decisions; **comply by 2027-01-01**.
- **Cybersecurity audits** — annual independent audit + CPPA certification, phased by revenue: **> $100M → 2028-04-01**; $50-100M → 2029-04-01; < $50M → 2030-04-01; annual after.

## Enforcement
- **1798.155** — Civil penalties up to $2,500 / $7,500 (intentional or minors). CPRA **removed the cure period as of right**. Enforced by CPPA + California AG.
